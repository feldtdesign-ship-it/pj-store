Snorton Salt Dad Hat

Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

At first glance, it’s innocent. A calm lavender dad hat, a raincoat, an umbrella, and a quiet moment. Look a little closer and Snorton Salt reveals itself as a masterclass in subtlety—a visual wink to indulgence, restraint, and the art of pretending everything’s perfectly normal.

 

This piece lives in the gray area between polite society and bad decisions made quietly. It’s satire at its most refined: nothing explicit, nothing shouted—just a gentle nod for those who catch it. Consider it seasoning, not the main course.

 

Soft, broken-in, and effortlessly wearable, the lavender wash keeps things light while the embroidered artwork carries the joke. If you know, you know. If you don’t… well, it just looks like a nice hat.

 

And yes—sometimes all it takes is a little bump in perspective.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

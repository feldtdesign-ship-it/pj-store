New York City Arteries Bucket Hats

Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York City Arteries turns the city’s lifeblood into a wearable statement. Inspired by the NYC subway map reimagined as a human heart, the embroidery maps boroughs as chambers & train lines as veins, capturing the motion, pressure, & pulse that keep New York alive.

Made from 100% cotton twill, this bucket hat balances structure with breathability. It’s soft enough for all-day wear, sturdy enough to hold its shape, & built to move easily between environments. Whether you’re navigating crowded sidewalks, riding trains end to end, or catching salt air at Jones Beach, it feels right where city grit meets open sky.

This is utility with meaning; designed to protect, circulate, & go wherever the day takes you.

Details

• 100% cotton twill

• Structured yet breathable construction

• Embroidered New York City Arteries heart-map graphic

• Comfortable fit for daily wear

• Ideal for city life, travel, & beach days

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

New York Fuckery Heavyweight Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York Fuckery takes the city’s chaos & stamps it into a cement grey garment dye seal you can wear; a parody of officialdom that got a little too real. The original drop sparked legal threats from the New York State Gaming Commission, who claimed the mark “diluted their well-known brand.”

A local attorney pushed back first, but things shifted when Covington & Burling, a global firm specializing in First Amendment protection; stepped in. Their position was simple:

this design is parody protected as free speech; from the MetroCard typography, to the “No trains, take the L” service-change graphics, to the subway car that always seems off-schedule, & of course… the word “Fuckery.”

After that response?

Silence.

Consider it a David vs. Goliath moment. New York style.

To honor the win, every New York Fuckery tee ships with an extra gift inside.

Wear it like a badge of honor, & a metaphorical middle finger to institutional bullying.

• 7.5oz heavyweight tee; garment-dyed cement grey

• Front hit + full back graphic in tonal black

• Premium print detail with a soft hand feel

• PJ original artwork; parody, resilience, & NYC attitude

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

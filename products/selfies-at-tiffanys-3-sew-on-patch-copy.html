Selfies at Tiffany's 3" sew on patch

$20

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

A quiet nod to elegance in a loud world. Selfies at Tiffany’s captures a moment of poise, contemplation, and modern femininity—stitched in crisp black and white with fine embroidered detail. At 2.5”, it’s subtle enough to feel refined, yet bold enough to be noticed by those who look twice.

This patch is fully embroidered and ready to be sewn on—designed to elevate whatever it touches.

Perfect to sew onto:

  • A black wool coat or tailored blazer lapel
  • A classic trench, peeking just above the pocket
  • A beret, beanie, or felt hat for understated charm
  • A canvas tote, linen bag, or silk-lined clutch
  • The inside lining of a jacket—hidden elegance, personal luxury
  • A vintage denim jacket to balance grit with grace

A small detail with a lot to say.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

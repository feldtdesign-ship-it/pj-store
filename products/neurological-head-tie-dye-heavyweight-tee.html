Neurological Head 1/1 Tie Dye Heavyweight Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

This is a 1/1 product made with extra love & a uniqueness that can’t be duplicated.

 “Neurological Head” maps the mind like a city; every pathway, memory, & electric impulse drawn as if you could trace thought with your finger. A full back illustration with microscopic detail reveals neural highways, cerebral texture, & patterned signal routes, while a subtle QR code sits behind the ear, leading deeper for anyone curious enough to see. 
Printed front + back in a high-precision single-color line style that stays clean & crisp on garment-dyed cement grey or jet black.

Details-

• 7.5oz heavyweight tee

• Premium print clarity with a soft hand feel

• Front signature hit + full back illustration

• PJ original artwork; introspective, architectural, anatomical

For the thinkers, overthinkers, & anyone who knows the mind has layers you can’t see; until now.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

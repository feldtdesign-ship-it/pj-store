The Bronx Bombers Heavyweight Crewneck carries New York power without raising its voice. PJ’s 2016 triple entendre; inspired by aviation muscle, stadium nights, street mythology, & the kind of confidence that doesn’t need explanation, the graphic hits with bold symmetry & layered meaning; iconic, grounded, & unmistakably New York.

Cut from a substantial 13.5 oz heavyweight fleece, this crewneck delivers real structure, warmth, & durability. The fabric feels dense yet comfortable, with a smooth cotton face that keeps the print crisp while wearing clean over time. Built to hold its shape, break in slowly, & age with character, it’s the kind of piece that earns its place in rotation.

Equally at home on cold platforms, late walks, ballpark exits, or everyday city movement, this crewneck balances toughness with polish. No flash. No excess. Just weight, intention, & presence.

New York energy—heavy, composed, & impossible to ignore.

Details-

  • 13.5 oz./yd² (US) / 22.5 oz./L yd (CA) heavyweight fleece
  • 70/30 ring-spun cotton / polyester 3-end fleece
  • 100% cotton face yarns on solid colors for premium print clarity
  • Double ribbing side panels for stretch & mobility
  • Reverse coverstitch sewing on all seams for durability & clean finish
  • Cross-grain body construction to reduce shrinkage after wash
  • 520gm 100% cotton 1x1 ribbing at cuffs & waistband
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

The Yellow Subway Line Premium Bomber Jackets

Color
Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

The Yellow Subway Line Bomber Jacket turns motion into uniform. Inspired by jolting platforms, late-night transfers, & the surreal rhythm of the city when everything feels slightly amplified, this piece channels the energy of a line that never really slows down. It’s psychedelic without being precious; playful without losing its edge.

Built on a classic bomber silhouette, the jacket balances utility & attitude. The water-resistant nylon shell shrugs off city weather, while the diamond-stitched interior adds warmth, structure, & comfort without bulk. Wrinkled sleeves give it a broken-in, lived-with feel; like something that’s already survived a few rides, sharp corners, & crowded cars.

Details like the zipped arm utility pocket, flapped hand pockets, & inner security pocket keep it practical, while the clean lines & tonal ribbing keep it sharp. It’s the kind of jacket that feels right on a platform, on the street, or cutting through the night when the city gets a little strange.

This isn’t nostalgia.

It’s movement, color, & controlled chaos; zipped up.

Details-

  • 3.1 oz./yd² 100% nylon shell with water-resistant finish
  • 5.3 oz./yd² 100% polyester diamond-stitched lining
  • Zip front closure
  • Zipped arm utility pocket
  • Slanted flapped pockets with push-stud fastening
  • Inner security pocket for essentials
  • Tone-on-tone ribbing at neck, cuffs, & hem
  • Wrinkled sleeves for a lived-in, street-ready look 
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

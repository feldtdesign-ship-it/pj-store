Selfies@Tiffany's Buttery Soft Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Selfies @ Tiffany’s on a buttery-soft white tee is understated confidence at its best. The graphic pulls from classic cinema glamour and reframes it for the era of reflection, self-awareness, & effortless cool. Timeless without feeling dated; playful without trying too hard.

The crisp white base keeps the look clean and iconic, letting the artwork breathe while pairing seamlessly with denim, tailoring, or layered city fits. Lightweight with a smooth, broken-in feel, this tee wears easy all day; polished enough to feel intentional, relaxed enough for everyday rotation.

Classic elegance. Modern attitude. Butter-soft comfort.

Features-

  • 4.3 oz lightweight fabric for exceptional softness & breathability
  • 60/40 ring-spun cotton / polyester sueded jersey
  • Laundered for a broken-in, buttery-soft feel
  • Clean white base for timeless contrast & clarity
  • Intricate single-color graphic with crisp line detail
  • Soft yet durable; built for everyday rotation
  • Classic fit with a clean, modern drape
  • Designed & printed with care
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

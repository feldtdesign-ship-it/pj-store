Niagra Falls Spa Signed Prints

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Niagra Falls Spa” transforms one of the world’s greatest natural wonders into one giant hydrotherapy session. After PJ and Jillian experienced the Niagra Falls boat tour together, PJ was inspired to turn the overwhelming mist, force, and chaos of the falls into something playfully relaxing. Lounging beneath the crashing water is a colossal PJ; the giant’s face is the artist’s own self-portrait. Equal parts travel poster, hippy era, mythological fantasy, and New York humor, the piece bridges New York and Ontario with one invitation: Come for the mist, stay for the schvitz.

Only 100 are printed & signed. 120 lb cardstock 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

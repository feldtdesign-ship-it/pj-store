Buggy to Cybercab Prints

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Buggy to Cybercab” captures the moment one era of transportation gives way to another. A family trades literal horsepower for electric power, stepping out of a horse-drawn carriage and into a futuristic autonomous vehicle. Centuries of innovation are separated by a single open door; one ride comes with one horse and a driver, while the other comes with hundreds of horsepower and no driver at all. The technology has changed beyond recognition, but the purpose remains the same: getting people from where they are to where they want to go.

Only 100 are printed & signed. 120 lb cardstock 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

New York City Arteries Crop Hoody

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York City Arteries is built as a quiet tribute to the people who keep the city alive—nurses on double shifts, cardiologists reading scans between coffees, doctors moving fast through hospital corridors while the city pulses outside. The graphic mirrors the truth every New Yorker knows: this city runs on arteries, avenues, subways, & heartbeats layered on top of one another.

Worn cropped & confident, this hoody balances strength & softness—practical enough for long days, expressive enough to carry meaning. It feels right outside a hospital entrance, on a walk between shifts, or grabbing air before heading back inside. The cropped silhouette keeps it modern & feminine, while the fabric has enough structure to hold its shape through real life, not just photos.

This isn’t medical cosplay.

It’s lived-in New York energy—where science meets soul, & care is part of the uniform.

For those who understand that saving lives & loving this city are both full-time jobs.

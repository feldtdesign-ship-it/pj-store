Apartments & Inflation Zip Midweight Hoody

Size

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Apartments & Inflation captures the quiet pressure of living in New York; the balancing act between shelter, cost, ambition, & survival. The graphic pulls from medical iconography & architectural form, turning buildings into anatomy and rent into something you feel in your chest. It’s a visual shorthand for the stress, resilience, & dark humor that come with calling this city home.

This Army Heather zip-up is built for daily wear in real New York conditions. At 8.5 oz., the midweight fleece lands right where comfort meets structure; warm enough for long walks & cold platforms, light enough to wear all day without feeling weighed down. It layers easily over a tee, zips clean against the wind, & stays comfortable from morning errands to late-night commutes.

The tone is understated & intentional. No noise, no excess; just a clear idea, worn quietly. It’s the kind of hoody that blends into the city while saying exactly what it needs to say.

Details-

  • 55/45 cotton/polyester
  • Classic fit
  • Jersey lined hood
  • Split-stitched double-needle sewing on all seams
  • Twill neck tape
  • 1x1 ribbing at cuffs & waistband
  • Metal eyelets
  • #5 metal kissing zipper
  • 8.5 oz./yd

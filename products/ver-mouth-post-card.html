Ver Mouth post card

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 “Ver Mouth” is a clever and playful homage to the classic French vermouth, infused with an Art Nouveau aesthetic. In this piece, the wordplay shifts Vermouth to Ver Mouth, highlighting the character’s exaggerated mouth as the focal point. The figure is a glamorous woman, drawn with elegant, fluid lines typical of the Art Nouveau style, characterized by her sweeping pose and flowing attire. The hat, feathered gown, and poised demeanor give off an air of sophistication, while her oversized lips and the play on words inject a sense of humor into the scene.

She holds a small glass of vermouth, balancing it gracefully, further reinforcing the connection to the drink, but the witty title and her smug, confident expression add a layer of lightheartedness. The use of minimal colors, with striking red lips and a white dress against a black background, allows the viewer to focus on the whimsical nature of the character.

This piece draws from the elegant, ornate style of early 20th-century French advertising posters, while reimagining it with a modern, cheeky twist that combines nostalgia and humor.

 


Postcard details:

5" x 7"

These vibrant 120 lb. cardstock postcards are sure to capture the eye and provide for a wonderful gift or salutations to a friend, co-worker, business, family member, or even a random Apple muncher.

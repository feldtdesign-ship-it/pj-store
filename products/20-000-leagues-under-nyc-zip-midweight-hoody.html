Hoodie

20,000 Leagues Under NYC Zip Midweight Hoody

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

20,000 Leagues Under NYC reimagines the daily commute as deep-sea exploration. The L train becomes a submarine; slipping beneath the city, cutting through tunnels like ocean trenches, carrying New Yorkers through darkness, pressure, & motion. What’s usually routine turns cinematic; a quiet adventure happening below the surface of the city.

The chest embroidery keeps the story subtle. A small signal rather than a shout; for those who know the ride, the delays, the rhythm of doors opening & closing underground. It reads clean on Army Heather; grounded, utilitarian, & unmistakably New York.

Built as a true midweight layer, this zip-up hoody hits the comfort sweet spot. At 8.5 oz., it’s warm without bulk, structured without stiffness; easy to wear on packed platforms, inside train cars, or walking home after midnight. Zipped up against cold air or worn open over a tee, it moves with you through real city days.

It’s transit mythology; worn quietly, understood instantly.

Details-

  • 55/45 cotton/polyester
  • Classic fit
  • Jersey lined hood
  • Split-stitched double-needle sewing on all seams
  • Twill neck tape
  • 1x1 ribbing at cuffs & waistband
  • Metal eyelets
  • #5 metal kissing zipper
  • 8.5 oz./yd²
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

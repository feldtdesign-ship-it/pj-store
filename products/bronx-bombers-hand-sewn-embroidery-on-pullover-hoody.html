Bronx Bombers Heavyweight Hoodies

Size
Weight

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

Bronx Bombers condenses New York power into a single, loaded image. It’s a triple entendre born in the city: graffiti writers bombing trains, a baseball dynasty built on pressure, & bomber aircraft engineered for force & inevitability. Different worlds, same energy.

This hoodie feels right moving through the Bronx—cutting across Mott Haven, posted up near 161st Street, or heading north toward Riverdale as the temperature drops. The city moves fast, the wind cuts sharper, & the layers matter. This one holds its ground.

Offered in two heavyweight black hoodie options, both are built for real use, cold weather, & long-term wear. Same artwork. Same presence. Two different weights, depending on how much armor you want.

The 10 oz. heavyweight balances warmth with mobility—substantial without bulk; easy to move in; ideal for daily wear, layering, & long hours outside.

The 13.3 oz. super heavyweight leans fully into structure & protection—denser, warmer, & more substantial in hand; built for colder nights, wind-exposed blocks, & standing still on late platforms.

New York confidence; reinforced.

Details-

  • Black heavyweight hoodies; two weight options
  • 10 oz./yd²; 70% cotton / 10% polyester / 20% recycled polyester
  • 13.3 oz./yd²; 60% cotton / 40% polyester
  • Heavy fleece with a structured, premium feel
  • Durable construction built for daily wear & cold conditions

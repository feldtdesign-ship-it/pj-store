A-Bombshell 1/1 Premium Heavyweight Ladies’ Tee

Size
Style

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

a true 1/1 never to be duplicated, one of PJ’s favorites in the catalog.

Chaotic, cinematic, & singular. A-Bombshell fuses mid-century glamour with atomic anxiety; an icon caught between spotlight & fallout. The familiar silhouette evokes 1950s cinema & flashing cameras, but here elegance fractures. A mushroom cloud erupts where a head should be, surrounded by concentric rings that suggest orbit, explosion, spectacle, & myth collapsing in on itself.

Printed in metallic silver ink, the linework shimmers against a deep black field; fluid, electric, & alive with motion. The figures repeat & stutter across the surface, creating a sense of instability & excess, as if fame itself is looping, multiplying, & detonating in real time. Sneakers anchor the imagery in the present, bridging postwar symbolism with modern street language.

This version is printed on an ultra-soft, lightweight tee with a buttery hand-feel & smooth drape. The laundered 60/40 ring-spun cotton/polyester sueded jersey wears effortlessly from day one, allowing the chaos of the artwork to contrast against a calm, luxurious touch.

A literal blonde bombshell; glamour weaponized, beauty irradiated, legend frozen at the moment of impact.

Features-

  • One-of-one release
  • 6 oz./yd² (US), 10 oz./L yd (CA), 100% Airlume combed & ring-spun cotton, 20 singles
  • Mid-length, boxy fit
  • Set-in sleeves
  • Double stitched neck
  • Side seams

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

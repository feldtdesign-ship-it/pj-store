Layer Cake of NY Gasoline Jackets

Size
Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

This jacket wears New York the way New York is built; layer by layer, under pressure, in constant motion.

The Layer Cake of New York artwork across the back is a full cross-section of the city; towers above, infrastructure & underground life exposed below. It’s not graphic decoration; it’s architectural storytelling. From first sketch to final stitch, PJ spent approximately 130 hours developing this design, obsessing over every layer, reference, & detail until the city read true.

Built from 12 oz. super-duty cotton duck & fully fleece lined, this Gasoline Jacket is made for real wear. Pleated elbows & an action back allow movement without restriction, while triple-needle stitched main seams reinforce every high-stress point. It’s structured, durable, & designed to age with character.

Utility is intentional & understated; two angled welt pockets, a right-chest zipper pocket, & three interior chest pockets keep essentials secure without clutter. Adjustable snap cuffs & side tabs let you fine-tune the fit depending on the season & the street.

This isn’t fashion for show; it’s infrastructure you can wear.

A city mapped, cut open, & rebuilt; 130 hours at a time.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

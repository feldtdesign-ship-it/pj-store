New York Fuckery Subtle Heavyweight Crewnecks

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Sometimes New York Fuckery doesn’t shout, it smirks.

This heavyweight classic crewneck is built for people who understand the city without needing it explained. Quietly funny, intentionally understated, & made with serious attention to quality, it’s the kind of piece that feels right moving through Penn Station. Coffee in hand, mind elsewhere; timing everything perfectly.

The design keeps things restrained; no back graphic, no excess. Just enough attitude to signal awareness, paired with a fabric weight & construction that does the real talking. It’s comfort with intention; humor with discipline; New York energy without the noise.

Dense, structured, & soft where it matters, this crewneck wears clean on its own or layered under a coat. It holds its shape, ages well, & looks better the more it becomes part of your routine.

This isn’t novelty.

It’s everyday fuckery; refined.

Details-

  • 13.5 oz./yd² (US); 22.5 oz./L yd (CA) heavyweight fleece
  • 70/30 ring-spun cotton/polyester; 3-end construction with 100% cotton face yarns
  • Double ribbing side panels for stretch & movement
  • Reverse coverstitch sewing on all seams for durability & a premium finish
  • Cross-grain body panels to reduce shrinkage & length loss
  • 520gm 100% cotton 1x1 ribbing at cuffs & waistband
  • Hand sewn; subtle execution; no back graphic

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

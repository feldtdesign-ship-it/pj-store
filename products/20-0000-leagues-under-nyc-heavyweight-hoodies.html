20,0000 Leagues Under NYC Heavyweight Hoodies

Size
Weight

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

20,000 Leagues Under NYC opens inside a subway car mid-crossing; straphangers gripping poles, faces lit by flickering fluorescents, eyes fixed on the windows as a giant squid drifts alongside in the East River. Tentacles slide past the glass, suction cups thudding softly against steel, the tunnel suddenly feeling less like infrastructure and more like open water. Inspired by Jules Verne & the L train’s underwater passage, the artwork turns a routine commute into a deep-sea encounter; fear, wonder, & disbelief sharing the same car.

Offered in two heavyweight hoodie options, both are built for cold platforms, late nights, & long exposure to wind that feels like it’s coming off the river itself. Same concept. Same presence. Two levels of armor.

The 10 oz. heavyweight delivers warmth with flexibility—soft, wearable, & easy to move in. It’s substantial without feeling bulky; ideal for everyday rotation, layering, & hours spent waiting underground while the city flows above.

The 13.3 oz. super heavyweight is built for maximum protection. Denser, heavier, & more structured, it’s designed for colder conditions, longer waits, & standing still while everything else moves. Premium feel throughout; no shortcuts.

Both versions feature the same 20,000 Leagues Under NYC artwork—bold, immersive, & designed to last. This isn’t just a hoodie. It’s survival gear for imagined depths.

Details-

  • Black heavyweight hoodies; two weight options
  • 10 oz. classic heavyweight; 70% cotton / 30% recycled polyester; soft cotton-faced finish
  • 13.3 oz. super heavyweight; 60% cotton / 40% polyester; maximum weight & structure
  • Built for cold weather, wind exposure, & long-term wear

New York City Arteries Premium Cotton Shorts

Color
Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York doesn’t sleep; it circulates. The New York City Arteries Shorts translate the city’s underground pulse into wearable form, mapping the five boroughs like a living system beneath the surface. Rendered in crisp white against a deep blue fleece, the graphic feels architectural, anatomical, & unmistakably New York.

Cut from a heavyweight yet breathable fleece with a smooth cotton face, these shorts balance structure & comfort. The relaxed fit & tapered knee opening give them an easy, intentional silhouette; built for movement, made for long days that turn into nights. Finished with jersey-lined hand pockets, a back pocket, & subtle construction details that reward a closer look.

This is city infrastructure, worn casually.

Details-

  • 8.5 oz./yd² (US) | 14.2 oz./L yd (CA) heavyweight fleece
  • 80/20 ring-spun cotton/polyester blend with 100% cotton face
  • 32 singles for a smooth, premium hand feel
  • Relaxed fit with tapered knee opening
  • Elastic waistband with shoestring drawcord
  • Sewn eyelets & sewn fly detail
  • Jersey-lined hand pockets + back pocket
  • Vibrant high-contrast NYC Arteries graphic
  • Sizes XS–XXL

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

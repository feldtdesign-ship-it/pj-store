New York Hemps Premium Beanies

Color

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Long ago on the tracks of the 7 train, a legend took shape.

Mr. Hemps became a folk hero of Queens; known for his bright orange cap, his giant rollups, generous spirit, & ability to lift moods wherever he went. From Queensboro Plaza to Main Street, he showed up with kindness, good vibes, & a smile, becoming a familiar presence to commuters, night riders, & anyone who needed a little light in their day.

No one knew where he came from or what he wanted in return, because he never asked for anything. His story rolled on like the 7 train itself; steady, communal, & unforgettable.

This neon orange beanie, made from 100% acrylic, pays tribute to that spirit; bold, warm, & impossible to miss.

Details-

  • 100% acrylic
  • Classic cuffed beanie fit
  • Warm, durable, & season-ready
  • Neon orange for maximum visibility

Layer Cake of NY Crop Hoodies

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Layer Cake of New York in soft pink reframes the city through a gentler lens; layered, complex, & quietly powerful. The illustration reveals New York in cross-section; iconic buildings rising above, subways, bridges, movement, & hidden stories stacked below. It’s a love letter to everything that lives beneath the surface while still reaching for the sky.

The cropped hoody silhouette keeps the piece light, modern, & feminine. At 8 oz., the fleece has just enough structure to hold its shape while staying breathable & comfortable. The pink tone softens the graphic, letting the detail feel playful, thoughtful, & wearable all at once.

This is the kind of hoody that feels perfect on a slow stroll from Washington Square Park up toward Union Square; coffee in hand, windows open, the city easing into a new season. Easy to throw on, easy to move in, & expressive without trying too hard.

New York isn’t just vertical.

It’s layered.

This is how you wear it.

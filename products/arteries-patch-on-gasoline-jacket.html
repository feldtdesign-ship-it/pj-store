New York City Arteries Gasoline Jackets

Size
Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

New York City Arteries was born on the Union Square L train platform, inspired by the giant subway map that once hung there. Walking past it daily, PJ found more than inspiration—those subway lines quite literally kept him alive. Selling prints & sketching underground built his independence, street smarts, & discipline in a way only New York could.

 

The design reshapes the NYC subway system into a human heart, with trains acting as blood pumping through the city’s veins—a tribute to the city’s pulse & survival.

 

The Gasoline Jacket is built for real use, not decoration. Heavy-duty construction, reinforced stitching, & rugged materials make it dependable in daily wear, cold weather, & unpredictable conditions. Designed with utility in mind, it delivers warmth without bulk, freedom of movement, & long-term durability—made to be worn hard & trusted everywhere.

The New York City Arteries embroidery is on the back, placed boldly at the center of the upper shoulders, with a PJ O’Rourke signature embroidery on the left chest & a smaller Arteries embroidery beneath it. Dense stitching & high thread count give the artwork lasting depth & presence.

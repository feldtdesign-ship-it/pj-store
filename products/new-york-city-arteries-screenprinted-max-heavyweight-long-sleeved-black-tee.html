New York City Arteries Heavyweight Long Sleeved Tee

Size

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York City Arteries is a best-selling, thought-provoking design by PJ O’Rourke that reimagines the NYC Subway Map as a human heart. The five boroughs become chambers; subway lines transform into veins & arteries; mapping the city’s lifeblood in a single image.

Inspired by years spent moving, creating, & surviving underground, the piece reflects PJ’s deep connection to the MTA. Early in his career, he sold art at the Union Square subway station; it was there that this design first took shape. What’s usually a map becomes something intimate & vital; a heartbeat you recognize if you’ve ever lived below street level.

Printed front & back in crisp single-color linework, the graphic reads like an X-ray of the city’s circulation. Worn at Queensboro Plaza on a windy day; trains rattling overhead, platforms buzzing, & the river air cutting through; the piece feels exactly where it belongs.

This isn’t decoration.

It’s infrastructure you wear.

Details-

  • 7.5 oz heavyweight tee; garment-dyed black 100% cotton
  • Soft hand print with high detail
  • Premium fit & feel with lived-in comfort
  • PJ original artwork; mapped from real subway lines

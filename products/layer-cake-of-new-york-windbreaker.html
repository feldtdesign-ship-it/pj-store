Layer Cake Of NY Windbreakers

Color
Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 The Layer Cake of New York Windbreaker treats the city like what it really is; a slice-by-slice construction of ambition, infrastructure, survival, & dreams piled on top of one another. Streets above. Subways below. Stories everywhere in between.

On the back, the full cake rises; layered, dense, & unmistakably New York. On the front chest, a single missing slice appears. Not lost. Just relocated. Proof that what’s missing from the whole is carried by the individual.

Designed for transitional weather, this lightweight, wind-resistant shell blocks sudden gusts, shrugs off light rain, & stays breathable as the day shifts. Available in black for a grounded, architectural feel & aqua for a sharper contrast; cooler, brighter, & slightly unexpected. Same structure. Different energy. Black lets the city loom. Aqua lets it breathe.

Because New York isn’t one thing.

It’s layers of movement.

Layers of pressure.

Layers of people carrying their piece of the city with them.

Sometimes you’re inside the cake.

Sometimes you’re the slice.

Either way, you’re part of the structure.

Details-

  • Lightweight 82gm polyester fabric
  • Interior water-resistant coating for surprise drizzle and city mist
  • Middle thru-pouch pocket for easy storage in the rain
  • Quarter zip
  • Matte finish with metal eyelets & zipper hardware
  • Fine mesh hood liner for breathability 
  • Elastic cuffs to keep wind where it belongs 
  • Locker loop at center back-hang it!
  • PJ O’Rourke original artwork, “Layer Cake of New York” — architecture, infrastructure, and the anatomy of a city

 

 

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

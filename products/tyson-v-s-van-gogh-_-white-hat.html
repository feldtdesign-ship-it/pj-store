Tyson vs. van Gogh Dad Hat

Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Tyson vs. van Gogh” by PJ O'Rourke, is a captivating fusion of the boxing & art worlds. Through skillful symbolism, it brilliantly juxtaposes the lives of boxing legend Mike Tyson & artist Vincent van Gogh, using the canvas & ear as potent icons of their experiences. This classic-style boxing poster pays homage to the resilience & artistry of these two iconic figures, masterfully crafted by the artist's deep admiration for their unique stories. 80% acrylic/ 20% cotton

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

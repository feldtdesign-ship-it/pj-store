Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

Print Details:

Our prints are a visual delight, bursting with an array of vibrant colors that breathe life into every design. Crafted with long-lasting inks, they retain their brilliance over time, promising that the artwork remains as captivating as the day it was printed. Each print is produced on robust 120lb paper, adding a touch of durability that not only enhances the aesthetic but also ensures your treasured art piece remains a striking and enduring part of your collection.

Specs & Sizes:

Sizes: 11" x 17", 16" x 24", 60" x 38"

Signed Prints:

For a truly unique touch, our prints are available with the artist's signature. Add an authentic signature to your chosen piece, transforming it into a one-of-a-kind masterpiece that bears the artist's personal mark of approval. It's an opportunity to connect with the art on a deeper level and own a piece of creativity that's uniquely yours.

Signed with a Message:

For an even more personalized touch, you have the option to request a short message from the artist along with their signature. Whether it's a heartfelt birthday wish, a romantic note for an anniversary, or a custom message for any special occasion, this unique addition makes your art a truly personal and memorable gift. Contact us directly at pj@pjorourkeii.com for more details on how to make your print even more meaningful and tailor-made for your specific moment.

Care:

Caring for your print is essential to ensure its lasting beauty. Keep it out of direct sunlight for extended periods to prevent fading, and regularly dust it to maintain its vibrancy. For added protection and a touch of elegance, framing is recommended to showcase your art piece at its best.

*Note: Frames are not included.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

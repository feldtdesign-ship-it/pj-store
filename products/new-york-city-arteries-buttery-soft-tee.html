New York City Arteries Buttery Soft Tees

Size
Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Royal blue & black suede tees

New York Arteries reimagines the city as a living body; its boroughs mapped like an anatomical heart, its subway lines & streets flowing like veins & capillaries. Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, & the Bronx are charted as vital organs, pulsing together in a system powered by movement, pressure, & persistence.

Rendered in intricate single-color linework, the illustration blends medical diagram, transit map, & hand-drawn mythology. Printed in crisp contrast on a deep royal blue tee, the design feels electric; technical yet organic, precise yet alive.

This version is printed on an ultra-soft, lightweight tee that trades weight for flow. The laundered 60/40 ring-spun cotton/polyester sueded jersey delivers a buttery hand-feel with a smooth drape that wears effortlessly from day one.

Features-

  • 4.3 oz lightweight fabric for exceptional softness & breathability
  • 60/40 ring-spun cotton / polyester sueded jersey
  • Laundered for a broken-in, buttery-soft feel
  • Royal blue base for bold contrast & line detail
  • Intricate single-color anatomical / cartographic artwork
  • Soft yet durable; built for everyday rotation
  • Classic fit with a clean, modern drape
  • Designed & printed with care
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

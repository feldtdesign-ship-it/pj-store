Bronx Bombers Windbreakers

Color
Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

The Bronx Bombers Windbreaker is built on a triple entendre that only works in New York:

graffiti writers who made the trains their canvas,

a baseball dynasty built on power & pressure,

& bomber jets designed to cut through air like inevitability.

This jacket lives at that intersection; where culture, motion, & force collide.

Designed for transitional weather, this lightweight, wind-resistant shell blocks sudden gusts, shrugs off light rain, & stays breathable when the pace changes. Available in classic camouflage for a rugged, industrial feel & navy for a cleaner, more disciplined silhouette. Two approaches. Same impact. The camo nods to underground movement & utility. The navy sharpens the reference; clean lines, heavy history.

Because bombing isn’t always about destruction.

Sometimes it’s about presence.

About showing up loud, unapologetic, & impossible to ignore.

From subway tunnels to stadium lights to high-altitude airspace, the message lands all the same.

Details-

  • Lightweight 82gm polyester fabric
  • Interior water-resistant coating for surprise drizzle & shifting conditions
  • Matte finish with metal eyelets & zipper hardware
  • Fine mesh hood liner for breathability when things heat up
  • Elastic cuffs to keep wind where it belongs (outside)
  • Locker loop at center back — hang it up between missions
  • PJ O’Rourke original artwork, “Bronx Bombers” graffiti, baseball, & aviation
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

PJ O'Rourke II

A-Bombshell Heavyweight Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

A-Bombshell captures a split second where elegance meets force. A iconic figure mid-twirl becomes a mushroom cloud; movement frozen at the exact moment beauty turns explosive. The linework stays delicate while the idea hits heavy; grace, power, & consequence sharing the same silhouette.

Printed large on the back of a black heavyweight tee, the artwork feels textured against the fabric with subtle puff paint. The puff gives the tee a slightly bumpy feel to it. 

This piece isn’t about shock value; it’s about control. The calm confidence of standing centered when everything else is volatile.

Built on a premium heavyweight cotton tee with a structured drape & durable print, it’s made to wear like a staple while carrying a message that lingers.

Details-

• Heavyweight cotton tee with structured, premium hand feel

• Full back A-Bombshell illustration with fine-line detail, PJ signature on chest

• Durable print designed to age cleanly over time

• Classic fit built for everyday wear

• PJ original artwork; beauty, power, & impact in balance

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

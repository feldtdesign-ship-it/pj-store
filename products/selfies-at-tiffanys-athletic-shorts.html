Selfies @ Tiffany’s Athletic Mesh Shorts bring polished attitude into peak-summer motion. Inspired by classic glamour reframed through a modern lens, the graphic plays with reflection, confidence, & effortless presence; scaled down, subtle, & intentionally placed. Quiet luxury energy, built for movement.

Designed for heat, motion, & long days that blur into nights, these shorts are cut from lightweight 100% polyester with a breathable mesh lining that keeps air flowing whether you’re working out, walking city blocks, catching sun by the water, or moving between plans without slowing down. They dry fast, move easy, & feel right from the first wear.

Equally at home in the gym, on the boardwalk, at the beach, or styled casually through the city, they balance athletic function with understated expression.

Details-

• 100% polyester shell with breathable mesh lining

• Lightweight & quick-drying

• Comfortable elastic waistband with drawstring

• Designed for training, swimming, lounging, & everyday summer wear

• Subtle Selfies @ Tiffany’s graphic detail

• Easy active fit built for heat & motion

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

Bronx Bombers lives at the intersection of extreme comfort & quiet flare. It’s built for movement, confidence, & moments when you don’t need to explain yourself; because the jacket already did. Whether you’re piloting a B-52 bomber jet, bombing late-night trains with blackout spray cans, or slipping out of the stadium to the sound of Frank Sinatra echoing through concrete corridors, this piece holds it all.

The name is a triple entendre; aviation, art, & baseball; because New York doesn’t speak in single meanings. Neither does this jacket. It balances class with edge, polish with rebellion, nostalgia with now. Familiar silhouette; zero nostalgia bait.

Graffiti was born in the Bronx; names cut into steel, ideas moving faster than permission, culture sprayed into existence while the city slept. This jacket salutes that era; when trains became canvases, tunnels became galleries, & rebellion learned how to move.

Lightweight yet warm, broken-in yet structured, it moves through the city like it belongs there; platforms, sidewalks, rooftops, bleachers. It doesn’t chase attention; it survives long enough to earn it.

This isn’t loud fashion.

It’s New York pressure; worn quietly, felt immediately.

A true city staple.

A bomber that fits New York like a glove.

  • 3.1 oz./yd², 100% nylon shell
  • Water-resistant exterior for everyday protection
  • 5.3 oz./yd², 100% polyester lining
  • Diamond stitched inner lining for comfort & structure
  • Stylish wrinkled sleeves for a lived-in, street-ready look
  • Zip front closure
  • Zipped arm utility pocket
  • Slanted flapped pockets with push-stud fastening
  • Inner security pocket for essentials
  • Tone-on-tone ribbing at neck, cuffs, & bottom hem

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

New York City Arteries Vest

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

Built on a true Workman silhouette, this vest is made for people who carry weight—physically, creatively, and mentally. The heavy canvas shell holds structure, while the insulated quilted lining delivers warmth without bulk. It’s tough enough for the job site, the studio, or the street, and refined enough to wear layered over a hoodie with the skyline behind you.

Like arteries in a body, the trains pump people through the city—moving energy, labor, ideas, and lives block by block, borough to borough. This vest maps that circulation in motion.

This piece isn’t decorative. It’s functional symbolism. A wearable diagram of how New York stays alive—and how you do too.

Details

  • 10 oz. 100% cotton Canyon Cloth™ canvas shell
  • 12 oz. snow-top polyester sherpa lining at collar and back yoke
  • Quilted nylon-lined body with 3 oz. polyfil insulation
  • Double chest patch pockets with snap closure
  • Triple-needle stitching at high-stress seams
  • Heavy-duty metal zipper with easy-grab puller
  • Interior cell phone pocket for everyday carry
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

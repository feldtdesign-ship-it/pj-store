New York Kicks Trucker Hat Orange

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Built for late nights, long roads, & city blocks that never sleep. The New York Kicks trucker hat blends classic truck-stop energy with downtown New York attitude; bright orange up front, blacked-out mesh in the back, & zero interest in playing it safe.

The graphic pulls straight from the streets: sneakers hanging off telephone wires, pigeons posted up like they own the block, & the constant grind of the city in motion. Somewhere in the middle of it all is a frustrated 16-wheel driver crawling through construction zones near Penn Station; horn tapped, patience gone, boxed in by cones, cabs, & concrete.

It’s a nod to hustle, territory, & getting where you’re going by any means; whether that’s a cross-country haul or a hard left off the FDR at 2 a.m.

Materials & Construction-

• Structured foam front panel with durable polyester face

• Breathable polyester mesh back panels

• Curved foam-reinforced visor with stitched detailing

• Adjustable plastic snapback closure

• High-density embroidered front patch

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

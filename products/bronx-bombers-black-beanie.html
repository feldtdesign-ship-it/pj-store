Bronx Bombers Premium Beanie

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

A triple entendre, fully realized.

Bronx Bombers brings together three New York power symbols in one vision: the Yankees, WWII bomber aircraft, & the fearless graffiti train writers of the 70s & 80s. Dreamed up in 2013 & completed in 2016, the design fuses sports legacy, military precision, & underground art culture into a single, vibrant statement.

It carries the confidence of Babe Ruth calling his shot in the 1932 World Series, the courage & accuracy of Tuskegee Airman Roscoe C. Brown taking down a jet fighter in 1945, & the bold, clean energy of early graffiti legends like ZEPHYR. Spray cans become bombs, subway platforms grow wings, & imagination takes flight.

The beanie itself is made from 100% acrylic, delivering lightweight warmth, durability, & a classic fit built for everyday wear.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

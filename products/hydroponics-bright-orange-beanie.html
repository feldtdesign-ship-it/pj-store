Hydroponics Premium Beanies

Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Built for growers of plants, ideas, and futures.

 Hydroponics draws inspiration from controlled environments, root systems, & the science of growth. It’s a nod to botany & cultivation; where patience, precision, & care turn small beginnings into something thriving. Whether you’re tending leaves or leveling up your own ecosystem, this piece is about nurturing what matters.

 Made with 100% recycled polyester, the ribbed knit offers a snug, reliable fit that holds heat & stands up to the seasons; indoors, outdoors, or under grow lights.

 For the growers. Always.

 Details-

  • Ribbed knit construction with a classic, snug fit
  • Made from 100% recycled polyester
  • Warm, durable, and season-proof
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

PJ O'Rourke II

The Yellow Subway Line Heavyweight Crewnecks

Size
Color

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

The Yellow Subway Line Heavyweight Crewneck distills motion, color, & controlled chaos into a quieter uniform. Inspired by jolting platforms, surreal commutes, & the dreamlike logic of moving through the city at speed, the graphic channels psychedelic optimism with a New York edge; playful, intense, & just a little disorienting in the best way.

This version keeps things restrained. The artwork is hand sewn on the chest, deliberately subtle, letting the weight & construction do the talking. No back graphic. No noise. Just a knowing signal for those who recognize the reference.

Built from a dense 13.5 oz heavyweight fleece, the crewneck delivers serious structure, warmth, & longevity. The smooth cotton face gives it a premium hand feel while the cross-grain construction helps it hold shape over time. It’s made to be worn hard, broken in slowly, & trusted when the temperature drops & the city feels sharp.

This is psychedelic energy, grounded.

A moving line, rendered still.

Details-

• 13.5 oz./yd² (US) / 22.5 oz./L yd (CA) heavyweight fleece

• 70/30 ring-spun cotton / polyester 3-end fleece

• 100% cotton face yarns on solid colors

• Double ribbing side panels for stretch

• Reverse coverstitch sewing on all seams for durability 

• Cross-grain body panels to limit shrinkage 

• 520gm 100% cotton 1x1 ribbing at cuffs & waistband

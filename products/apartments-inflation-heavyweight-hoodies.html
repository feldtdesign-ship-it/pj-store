Apartments & Inflation Heavyweight Hoodies

Size
Weight

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Apartments & Inflation is a visual receipt for living in New York. Rising numbers, shrinking space, rent hikes disguised as “upgrades”—this piece captures the quiet math every New Yorker does just to stay put. It’s not commentary from the outside; it’s lived experience stitched into fabric.

The hoodie is offered in two heavyweight options, both built to hold up through long winters, colder apartments, & nights spent questioning where the heat went. The 10 oz. heavyweight balances warmth with flexibility—substantial enough for everyday wear, easy to move in, & built for constant rotation. The 13.3 oz. super heavyweight turns the dial up; denser, more structured, & designed for maximum insulation when the city feels especially expensive.

This is comfort armor. Something solid to wear while everything else keeps climbing.

Details-

  • Black heavyweight hoodies; two weight options
  • 10 oz. classic heavyweight; 70% cotton / 30% recycled polyester; soft cotton-faced finish
  • 13.3 oz. super heavyweight; 60% cotton / 40% polyester; maximum weight & structure
  • Built for cold weather, wind exposure, & long-term wear

New York Fuckery athletic shorts

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Built for heat, motion, & the kind of summer days that refuse to stay in one place. The New York Fuckery Shorts move easily from sand to pavement to hardwood; Coney Island mornings, sweaty gym sessions, & pickup runs at Rucker Park when the court’s loud & the sun won’t quit.

Lightweight & fast-drying, they’re made for jumping in the water, sprinting back on defense, or cooling off on a bench between runs. The elastic waistband keeps things comfortable when the pace changes, while the mesh lining breathes through long games & longer days.

Finished with a subtle embroidered New York Fuckery hit: quiet, observant, & very intentional. These aren’t performance theater shorts. They’re real-life shorts for real movement.

Details-

• 100% polyester shell with breathable mesh lining

• Lightweight & quick-drying

• Comfortable elastic waistband with drawstring

• Designed for basketball, running, swimming, & everyday summer movement

• Subtle embroidered New York Fuckery graphic

• Easy active fit built for heat & motion

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

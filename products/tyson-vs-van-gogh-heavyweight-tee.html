Tyson vs. van Gogh Heavyweight Tee

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Tyson vs. van Gogh” is the loudest fight that never happened: a vintage boxing poster reimagined as a cultural collision. Promoted like a once-in-a-lifetime prizefight, this piece pits raw physical dominance against artistic madness, turning the canvas into a ring & the ring into a work of art.

From the roaring crowd to the ringing bells, the hype is half the fight. You’ll be up to your ears in entertainment before the first punch is thrown; ears ringing, ears burning, ears barely hanging on. Two people’s champions. Two unmistakable geniuses. One surreal, unsanctioned spectacle the world was never meant to see again.

Gates open 6pm.

Brooklyn Bridge Park.

DUMBO, Brooklyn.

No Internet. No TV. No Radio.

Printed front & back on a premium heavyweight tee that gives the artwork the presence it deserves.

  • 7.5oz heavyweight cotton tee: garment-dyed natural
  • Full back vintage-style fight poster + front chest hit
  • Durable print with a soft hand feel
  • PJ original; art history meets heavyweight hype
Shipping and returns

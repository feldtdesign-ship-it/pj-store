The Illest 15” sew on patch

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

 

A frozen moment of greatness rendered as iconography. This piece captures dominance, endurance, and unity at the peak of competition—where legacy is earned through pain, trust, and triumph.

The symbolism within the artwork, including the number one representing his unparalleled greatness and the reference to "Brooklyn's Finest” with Al Capone (Brooklyn boy, took over Chicago) and Notorious BIG (“Brooklyn’s Finest”) adds layers of depth and meaning to a piece that resonates with sports and art enthusiasts alike. 

 
15” x 9.75” embroidered patch 

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

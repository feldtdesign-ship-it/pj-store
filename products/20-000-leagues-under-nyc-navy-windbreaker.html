PJ O'Rourke II

20,000 Leagues Under NYC Windbreaker

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

20,000 Leagues Under NYC reimagines the L train as a submarine, slipping beneath the East River where tunnels feel alive & infrastructure moves with pressure & purpose. Inspired by deep-sea mythology & late-night subway reality, the artwork turns a daily commute into an expedition; where headlights cut through darkness & the city keeps moving, unseen.

Designed for transitional weather, this lightweight navy windbreaker blocks sudden gusts, shrugs off light rain, & stays breathable on the move. Built for the streets above ground, while honoring the systems working below.

Because beneath the skyline is another city; powered by momentum, pressure, & people trusting the tunnel to get them through.

Details-

  • Lightweight 82gm polyester shell
  • Middle thru-pouch pocket for easy storage in the rain
  • Quarter zip
  • Interior water-resistant coating for light rain & mist
  • Matte navy finish with metal eyelets & zipper hardware
  • Fine mesh hood liner for breathable comfort
  • Elastic cuffs to keep wind out
  • Locker loop at center back
  • PJ O’Rourke original artwork, 20,000 Leagues Under NYC
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

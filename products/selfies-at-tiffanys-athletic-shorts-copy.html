Brooklyn Cassettes Athletic Shorts bring analog energy into peak-summer motion. Rooted in rewind culture, the cassette patch is a quiet nod to repetition, rhythm, & putting the work in—whether that’s music, movement, or miles logged through the borough.

Built for heat, sweat, & long days outside, these shorts are cut from lightweight 100% polyester with a breathable mesh lining that keeps air moving when the pace picks up. They dry fast, move easy, & stay comfortable whether you’re training hard or just stacking steps.

They feel right jogging through Prospect Park, lifting in a no-frills gym in Bushwick, shooting hoops in Bed-Stuy, or cutting over to Williamsburg for a cooldown walk by the water. Summer in Brooklyn doesn’t slow down; these keep up.

Functional, understated, & built for repetition.

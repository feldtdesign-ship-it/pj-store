Tyson vs. van Gogh Premium Beanie

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Two legends. Two kinds of genius.

Tyson vs. van Gogh brings the worlds of boxing and art into a classic fight-poster showdown; power versus vulnerability, instinct versus expression. Iconic symbols collide, drawing parallels between the raw intensity of Mike Tyson & the tortured brilliance of Vincent van Gogh.

It’s a tribute to resilience, creativity, & the thin line between chaos and greatness.

Made from 100% acrylic, this beanie delivers lightweight warmth, durability, & a clean everyday fit.

Layer Cake of New York Buttery Soft Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Crispy white-

Layer Cake of New York slices the city open to reveal what it’s truly made of—skyscrapers rising above, subway arteries pulsing below, and countless lives stacked in between. Rendered as a cross-section that feels part blueprint, part birthday cake, the design captures New York as both machine and celebration.

 

Printed in a detailed single-color line style, the artwork carries architectural weight with a hand-drawn pulse. Set against a clean white tee, the illustration reads crisp and dimensional, allowing every line and layer to breathe.

 

Printed on an ultra-soft, lightweight white tee, this version trades heaviness for flow. The laundered 60/40 ring-spun cotton/polyester sueded jersey delivers a buttery hand-feel with a smooth drape that wears effortlessly from the first day.

 

 

Features

 

 

  • 4.3 oz lightweight fabric for superior softness and breathability
  • 60/40 ring-spun cotton / polyester sueded jersey
  • Laundered for a broken-in, buttery-soft feel
  • Clean white base for sharp contrast and line detail
  • Front + back print in single-color linework
  • Soft yet durable — designed for everyday wear
  • Classic fit with an easy, modern drape
  • Designed and printed with care

 

 

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

New York Hemps (2015)

In a game that will go down in the annals of sports history, the Dank Trees faced off against their fierce cross-town rivals in what can only be described as a “Dubway Series” classic. The anticipation was palpable as Mr. Hemps, Mike Pizza, and Darryl Sugared Strawberries stepped up to the plate, each of them bringing their A-game to the field.

Mr. Hemps, the standout of the night, continued his career-best season by leading the charge. His blunt bat was on fire, and the crowd was treated to a power display that included multiple home runs. Mike Pizza and Darryl Sugared Strawberries followed suit, each contributing to the onslaught with their own homers, leaving the opposition in the dust.

The final score, fittingly 4/20, reflected the dominance of the Hemps. Their hits were plentiful, and their energy was unmatched. The dugout, always a place of camaraderie and tradition, was lit—literally—as they celebrated their victory in the spirit of their legendary rival, The Sultan of Pot.

The rivalry might be old, but the Hemps’ performance showed they’re anything but outdated. This blowout victory not only solidifies their place in the series but also serves as a reminder that when these teams blaze, history is made.

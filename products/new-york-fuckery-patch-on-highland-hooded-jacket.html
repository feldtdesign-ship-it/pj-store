New York Fuckery Heartland Hooded Coat

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

The New York Fuckery Heartland Hooded Coat is built for the parts of the city that don’t run on schedules- or explanations. Sidewalks that fight back. Corners where the wind hits sideways. Days that start early & end with paperwork you didn’t ask for.

This is the jacket you wear while reading a cease & desist letter from the New York State Gaming Commission, standing in the cold, or strolling past the chess players at Union Square; watching strategy unfold while realizing the city will always test you, but rarely break you. Heavy, dependable, & unbothered, it’s rooted in classic American workwear & sharpened by real New York experience.

Quilt-lined for warmth, triple-stitched where it counts, & cut with room to move, this is heritage gear designed for modern fuckery: built to last longer than the arguments against it.

Made from 12 oz. super-duty 100% cotton duck with a heavyweight quilt-lined interior, this jacket delivers serious warmth, structure, & durability. Finished with a three-piece insulated hood, heavy-duty brass zipper, & triple-needle stitched seams, it’s designed to feel substantial, protective, & built to last.

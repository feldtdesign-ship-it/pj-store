Apartments & Inflation Premium Beanies

Color

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Apartments & Inflation is a tongue-in-cheek parody of the New York City Department of Sanitation; because in this city, rising rent & rising prices feel like hazards you deal with daily. The familiar civic seal gets reworked into a wearable warning label, stamped right on your head for maximum visibility.

The ribbed knit delivers a classic, no-nonsense fit; structured, warm, & built for everyday wear. Available in bottle green, black, mustard yellow, & light grey, it covers the full New York spectrum; from workwear to winter uniform.

Made from 100% recycled polyester, this beanie literally gives trash a second life. Old plastic bottles, new purpose. Consider it sanitation with a sense of humor.

It’s sustainable, practical, & quietly sarcastic; just like surviving New York, heading over the Queensboro Bridge.

Details-

  • Ribbed knit construction for a snug, everyday fit
  • Available in bottle green, black, mustard yellow, & light grey
  • Made from 100% recycled polyester
  • Soft, durable, & cold-weather ready
  • Sustainability note; yes, it’s made from recycled bottles; because someone had to clean up the mess
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

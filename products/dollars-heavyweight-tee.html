Dollars Heavyweight Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Dollars” turns Benjamin Franklin into a modern myth; beaming down currency like sunlight. Equal parts optimism and satire, the piece plays with America’s favorite symbol of success, asking whether money guides us… or blinds us.

Minimal by design, the graphic hits with clarity & confidence: bold script, radiant lines, & Franklin floating somewhere between patron saint and warning sign.

Printed front & back in a crisp single-color process on a premium heavyweight tee that feels substantial without sacrificing comfort.

 Details-

  • 7.5oz heavyweight cotton tee, garment-dyed natural
  • Soft hand feel with durable print
  • Front statement graphic + smaller back hit
  • PJ original artwork, clean, iconic, & ironic
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

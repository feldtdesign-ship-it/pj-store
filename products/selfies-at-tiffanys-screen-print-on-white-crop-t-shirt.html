Selfies@Tiffany's Crop Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Selfies @ Tiffany’s is a modern nod to timeless style—where old-school elegance meets right-now attitude. The graphic pulls from classic cinema energy & flips it for the age of mirrors, moments, & main-character confidence. It’s playful without being loud, iconic without trying too hard.

Cut cropped for a flattering, feminine fit, this tee feels effortless; perfect with denim, layered under a jacket, or styled as a standalone statement. Soft, breathable, & easy to wear, it’s the kind of piece that feels polished but never precious.

Classic glamour. Modern perspective. No filter needed.

Details-

  • 100% ringspun cotton
  • Lightweight, ultra-soft hand feel
  • Feminine cropped silhouette
  • Smooth surface for crisp, lasting print
  • Designed for everyday wear with elevated attitude
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

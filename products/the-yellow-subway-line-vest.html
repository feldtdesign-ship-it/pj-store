The Yellow Subway Line Vest

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“The Yellow Subway Line; a psychedelic Liverpool meets Ed Sullivan theatre meets the sixties meets Peter Maxx meets acid trips & colorful (N)ightmares. Frights on jolting, speeding & brake slamming by blue meanie train conductors on crowded Qs. This workman vest is a great garment to be wearing to “weather” the bumps & bruises against cartoon villains on your way to the Upper East Side.

From downtown platforms to Astoria, this is protection against cold air, sharp corners, & whatever strange character ends up standing too close to you on the train.

Details-

  • 10 oz. 100% cotton Canyon Cloth™ canvas
  • 12 oz. snow top polyester sherpa lined collar & back yoke

Features-

  • Double chest patch pocket with snap closure
  • Quilted nylon-lined body with 3 oz. of polyfil insulation
  • Triple-needle stitching to reinforce high stress seams
  • Heavy-duty metal zipper with easy grab puller
  • Inside cell phone pocket
  • R is for Ringo 
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

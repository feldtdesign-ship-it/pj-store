Not Ready to Die (2024)

Donald Trump, now known as "The Notorious DJT,"  has released his first mixtape since the assassination attempt, titled "Not Ready to Die." The title is a tribute to Notorious B.I.G.'s iconic 1994 album Ready to Die, coinciding with the 30th anniversary of its release. The mixtape’s tracks eerily mirror the original tracklist, with titles like "Who Shot Ya," "Machine Gun Funk," "Unbelievable," and "One More Chance," all alluding to the events of July 13th.

In this album, Trump channels the style of Christopher Wallace, the legendary Brooklyn MC better known as Notorious B.I.G. By making these connections, Trump not only pays homage to the iconic rapper but also weaves his own near-death experience into the fabric of hip-hop history, aligning his personal narrative with the legacy of one of the genre's most influential figures.

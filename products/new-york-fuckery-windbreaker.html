New York wind doesn’t care about forecasts. It turns corners into wind tunnels, scaffolding into sails, & umbrellas into broken promises.

The New York Fuckery Windbreaker is built for those surprise gusts that hit hardest exactly when you step out of the subway. One minute it’s calm. The next, you’re wrestling a trash bag like it owes you money.

Lightweight but prepared, this jacket is for the in-between seasons; the days when the weather, like the city, refuses to make up its mind. It blocks the bite, sheds the drizzle, & looks official enough that no one questions why you’re standing there bracing yourself.

Because in New York, the wind is just another system that doesn’t work the way it’s supposed to.

Details-

  • 82gm lightweight polyester fabric
  • Interior water-resistant coating 
  • Middle thru-pouch pocket for easy storage in the rain
  • Quarter zip
  • Matte finish with metal eyelets & zippers
  • Fine mesh hood liner for breathability
  • Elastic cuffs to keep the wind where it belongs
  • Locker loop at center back — hang it up when the storm passes
  • PJ original artwork; weather, infrastructure, & everyday Fuckery
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

New York City Arteries Premium Beanies

Arteries reimagines the NYC Subway Map as a heart, celebrating PJ O’Rourke’s deep connection to the subway & the MTA’s impact on his life. Shifting NYC’s boroughs into a heart shape, the subway lines become intricate veins & arteries, highlighting the city’s lifeblood. This art piece underscores the subway’s crucial role in NYC’s vitality & invites viewers to appreciate its heartbeat.

This well-structured beanie is built with intention; thick, durable, & architectural. The Blackout Grey palette lets the embroidered artery patch speak quietly but powerfully; like a pulse beneath concrete. Black is a classic go to. Inspired by the subway system as the city’s circulatory system, the design maps NYC as a living organism; alive, layered, & relentless.

Crafted from 100% acrylic, the knit holds its shape while delivering warmth without bulk. The structure gives it a clean silhouette whether worn cuffed or low; making it a true everyday piece with backbone.

Details-

  • 100% acrylic
  • Blackout Grey & Black colorway
  • Premium embroidered NYC Arteries patch
  • Dimensions; 12.5” H x 16.5” W
  • Durable, warm, & built to last

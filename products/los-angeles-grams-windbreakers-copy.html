Snorton Salt Subtle Windbreaker

Color
Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

The Snorton Salt windbreaker is built for weather that can’t make up its mind, & for people who already have. Lightweight, clean, & quietly mischievous, it’s the kind of layer you throw on when the forecast says maybe & the mood says definitely.

The matte finish keeps things understated while the interior water-resistant coating handles surprise rain without drama. Wind stays out. Air moves through. You stay dry enough, cool enough, and just suspiciously comfortable. The chest patch does the talking; small, graphic, & loaded with implication.

There’s plenty of salt in the air here.

Sea spray. Street grit. Seasoning you don’t ask about.

Take it literally if you want. Or don’t.

Details-

• Lightweight 82gm polyester shell

• Interior water-resistant coating for drizzle & mist

• Matte finish with metal eyelets & zipper hardware

• Fine mesh hood liner 

• Elastic cuffs to keep wind where it belongs (outside)

• Locker loop at center back-hang it up when the day resets

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Screen printed with pink puff ink on a heavyweight, garment-dyed 100% cotton 7.5 oz blank.

“Wuhansel & Gretel” twists a classic fairytale into a modern cautionary tale; two kids searching for safety, breadcrumbs replaced by face masks, & the woods standing in for the uncertain world we all walked through. The candy house is still there, but the sweetness carries a warning; nothing tempting comes without risk.

Printed oversized on the front in a bold violet single-color process that pops against the garment-dyed heavyweight cement grey, with a back signature hit to finish it clean.

• 7.5oz heavyweight tee; garment-dyed cement grey

• Oversized front illustration + back PJ signature

• Premium print detail with soft hand feel

• PJ original artwork; pandemic-era folklore with bite

For anyone who made it through the woods & knows sometimes survival means rewriting the story.

Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

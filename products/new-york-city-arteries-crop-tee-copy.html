Brooklyn Cassettes Crop Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Brooklyn Cassettes is a love song to analog days; when music lived in pockets, playlists were handmade, & rewinding took patience.

The graphic nods to old-school tapes, vinyl crates, liner notes, & the ritual of pressing play with intention. Set in a bold Swordfish-style font, the lettering feels sharp, timeless, & slightly rebellious; like album art you’d recognize from across the room. It carries that perfect mix of toughness & softness that Brooklyn has always done best.

Cut into a feminine crop, the tee feels easy, flattering, & lived-in from the first wear. Lightweight & breathable, it sits comfortably on the body without trying too hard; made for walking tree-lined blocks, stopping into record shops, & catching afternoon sun on a stoop.

Brooklyn in the spring, windows open. Music drifting out onto the sidewalk. Layers coming off. The city warming back up & finding its rhythm again. This is the kind of tee you throw on without thinking; because it already feels like part of you.

Details

  • 100% ringspun cotton
  • Soft, breathable feel with a smooth finish
  • Feminine cropped silhouette
  • Lightweight & easy for warm days or layered nights
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

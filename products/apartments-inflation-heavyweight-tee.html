Apartments & Inflation Heavyweight Tee

Size

Ships in 2 to 3 days from New York. Prints go flat, in a tube.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

“Apartments & Inflation” turns a familiar NYC municipal seal into something a little too real: the medicine every New Yorker swallows to stay here. The skyline stacks like prescription pills, & the caduceus replaces snakes with intertwined rat tails; because nothing represents the city’s survival instinct better than the creatures that never leave.

Printed front & back in a crisp single-color line style that feels official at first glance… until you read between the lines.

 Details-

  • 7.5oz heavyweight tee; black
  • Premium print detail with soft hand feel
  • Full front graphic + back hit with PJ signature
  • PJ original artwork; parody rooted in NYC truth
Shipping and returns

Prints ship flat in a tube. Garments ship in 2 to 3 business days. Unworn pieces can come back within 14 days.

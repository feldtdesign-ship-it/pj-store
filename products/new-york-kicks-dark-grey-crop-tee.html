New York Kicks Crop Tee

Size

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

New York Kicks reclaims street energy through a feminine lens; confident, effortless, & unapologetically at ease. Cropped just right; it sits with intention; showing strength without forcing it. The graphic pulls from classic New York iconography; sneakers laced tight; city pride worn loud; but the fit tells a different story; lighter, freer, & made to move with you.

This isn’t oversized bravado or borrowed masculinity; it’s New York style distilled; cool without trying; tough without hardness; expressive without noise. The crop hits clean at the waist; balancing edge with elegance; whether you’re walking through a park; ducking into a café; or owning the sidewalk without saying a word.

Soft to the touch but strong in presence; this tee feels lived-in from the first wear. It’s the kind of piece that doesn’t ask for attention; but always gets it.

Details-

  • 100% ring-spun cotton
  • Soft, breathable feel with a natural drape
  • Feminine cropped silhouette
  • Lightweight, easy, & street-ready
  • Designed for movement, confidence, & everyday wear

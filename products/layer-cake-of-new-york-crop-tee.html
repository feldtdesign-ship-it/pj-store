Layer Cake of NY Crop Tee

Size

Ships from New York.

Signed editions are numbered by hand and close at 100, 25 and 5.

Layer Cake of New York reveals the city the way it’s actually lived; stacked, layered, chaotic, & beautiful. The illustration cuts New York in cross-section, showing iconic buildings above ground & the hidden systems below; subways, tunnels, movement, & stories all coexisting in one vertical slice.

Printed on a crisp white 100% ringspun cotton crop tee, the contrast feels light, graphic, & intentional. The cropped silhouette keeps it modern & feminine, while the artwork carries depth; playful at first glance, thoughtful the longer you look. It’s the kind of piece that balances softness with structure, ease with meaning.

This tee feels right walking through SoHo on a sunny afternoon, sitting on the steps in Union Square, biking along the West Side Highway, or wandering Greenwich Village where old New York still peeks through. It works just as naturally in front of historic facades, brownstones, & corner cafés as it does layered into everyday city life.

New York isn’t flat.

It’s layered.

This is the slice you wear.

Details

  • 100% ringspun cotton
  • Soft, breathable feel with a smooth finish
  • Feminine cropped silhouette
  • Lightweight & easy for warm days or layered looks
  • Original Layer Cake of New York artwork
